My story

A non-linear path from engineering and cycling to cybersecurity.

My curiosity about technology started early. Around 2008, I opened TV remotes to understand how they communicated wirelessly. Later, I explored Bluetooth phone features, Android rooting, and custom ROMs.

I completed SSC (10th grade) in 2009 with 63.07%, followed by a three-year Diploma in Electronics and Communication at VES Polytechnic (2010–2013), where I earned 73.43%.

My engineering route was uneven. I began direct second-year BE studies at RGIT in 2012, continued through readmission to VESIT in 2013, and returned to VESIT again in 2015. I stepped away from engineering to pursue competitive road cycling for a year in 2014, and then for around 2.5 years from 2016 to 2018. I eventually completed the final year of my BE in Electronics and Telecommunication at VESIT in November 2020, with a 5.86 CGPA (55.76%).

I worked part-time at Decathlon Sports India from January to December 2019 while completing my degree. Cycling has remained a major part of my life, including competitive races and a first-place finish in an Individual Time Trial in Bengaluru in January 2026, along with third place in two other events.

From March 2021, my professional work included IT audit at EY, Goldman Sachs, and KPMG, followed by a GRC role at TechD Cybersecurity in 2026. In 2026, my interest shifted more directly toward cybersecurity practice. I have started an MS in Computer Science with a focus on cybersecurity at SRH University of Applied Sciences, Heidelberg, Campus Leipzig.
