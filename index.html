My story

A non-linear path to cybersecurity.

I studied Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering at VESIT, but my interest increasingly leaned toward computer science and software. Around the same period, competitive road cycling became a major part of my life, and I spent four years pursuing it seriously.

I later worked in IT audit for more than four years. That experience gave me a foundation in IT controls, risk assessment, testing, and documenting evidence. Over time, I realised I wanted to work more directly with technology and investigate security problems, rather than focus mainly on audit and compliance.

I’m now making a deliberate move into cybersecurity. In October 2026, I plan to begin my MS in CyberSecurity at SRH University in Germany. I want to build deeper technical skills and work toward AI-assisted vulnerability research, application security, and ethical hacking.

I’m especially interested in how AI can help explore code and suggest vulnerability hypotheses. For me, those suggestions are a starting point: security claims still need to be checked through authorised testing and supported by evidence.
