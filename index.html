About me

From IT audit to penetration testing.

My background is in IT audit, IT general controls, and risk and compliance work. I have supported control testing, risk assessments, audit documentation, and remediation tracking across financial-services environments.

I’m focused on penetration testing and application security. I want to understand how applications behave, investigate potential weaknesses, and explain supporting evidence and possible impact clearly.

I also have a long-standing interest in Android devices, including ADB, Fastboot, bootloader unlocking, rooting, and custom ROMs. I use AI as a research assistant to explore code and possible explanations, but I treat its output as a starting point rather than proof of a vulnerability.

How I approach security testing

I start with a clear security question and scope. In authorised testing, I investigate behaviour, check potential findings against evidence, consider impact, and research remediation. AI can help me explore code and develop hypotheses, but I do not treat its output as proof.
