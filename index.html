My career direction

A non-linear path to cybersecurity.

I studied Electronics and Telecommunication (EXTC) Engineering at VESIT, but my interest leaned more toward computer science and software. During my engineering years, I spent around four years seriously pursuing competitive road cycling.

I then spent more than four years working in IT audit. That experience gave me a foundation in IT controls, risk assessment, testing, and documenting evidence. Over time, I realised that a career focused mainly on audit and compliance was not the right long-term fit for me: I wanted to work more directly with technology, test how systems behave, investigate weaknesses, and understand how to fix them.

I’m now taking a deliberate step toward cybersecurity by moving to Leipzig, Germany, for the October 2026 intake of the M.Sc. Computer Science – Cyber Security programme at SRH University. My goal is to grow into AI-assisted vulnerability research, application security, and ethical hacking.

I see AI as a way to explore code, develop hypotheses, and research possible fixes—not as proof that a vulnerability exists. I want this website to become a record of practical work, authorised testing, clear reports, and lessons learned as I build this next chapter.
